# Rating matrix: Hardware Wallet Comparison

English transcription of the LIGHT UP infosheet (German original: [So testen wir](https://lightupkryptos.de/so-testen-wir/)). The wallet track uses it only as a starting point: teams may borrow from it or design a completely new matrix for software wallets.

A multi-coin wallet can reach 100 points; rating = points ÷ 10. Bitcoin-only wallets are rated without the categories asset support and features, out of 80 points; rating = points ÷ 8. The two series are not comparable with each other.

## Security · 60 points

| Criterion | Scoring | Max. |
|---|---|---|
| Registered office | DACH, Luxembourg, Liechtenstein: full points · Europe: 6 · USA and Canada: 4 · other reputable jurisdictions: 2 · offshore jurisdictions: 0 | 8 |
| Year founded | Before 2016: full points · after 2024: 0 · 2016 to 2024: pro rata | 12 |
| Data retention | Under 1 month: 4 · 1–6 months: 2 · 6–12 months: 1 · over 12 months: 0 | 4 |
| Trustpilot | Points based on rating and number of reviews | 6 |
| Open source | Open-source software: 6 · open-source hardware: 6 | 12 |
| Secure element chip | EAL6+ or higher: full points · EAL5+: 4 · another chip: 2 · none: 0 | 5 |
| Two-factor authentication | Yes: full points · no: 0 | 2 |
| Multi-signature | Yes: full points · no: 0 | 2 |
| Passphrase | Yes: full points · no: 0 | 2 |
| Authenticity check | Yes: full points · no: 0 | 2 |
| All security-relevant input on the device | Yes: full points · no: 0 | 4 |
| Quantum-ready | Yes: full points · no: 0 | 1 |
