# Crypto Guide for Beginners

**Guiding question:** How do you explain Bitcoin and crypto to someone who knows nothing about them?

## Starting point

LIGHT UP has published more than 700 YouTube videos since 2021, each written out word for word. Andi and Peed are now writing a book for beginners. After so many videos, they are looking for angles they have not tried yet: a different first question, an everyday comparison, an exercise that makes an idea click.

## Your task

Find new ways into the topic and test whether they work.

1. **Write individually first (15 minutes).** Each team member drafts a short [learning path](../templates/learning-path.md) for a specific beginner they have in mind. Describe the reader without naming them.
2. **Compare.** Where do your paths start? Which prerequisite do several of you agree on, and where would reasonable people teach things in a different order?
3. **Collect angles.** For each promising angle, note the reader's question, the explanation or comparison, an exercise and a way to check that the reader understood.
4. **Write one sample.** Develop your strongest angle into a two-page spread as it would appear in a book: explanation, example and a small exercise.
5. **Test it.** Ask a peer from another team to read the spread and do the exercise. Mark the first term they did not understand, and revise one passage.

## What LIGHT UP gets out of it

New angles, explanations and exercises that can flow into the book. If a contribution shapes the book significantly and its author wishes, LIGHT UP names them in the book.

## What to submit

Use the [guide team template](../templates/guide-team.md):

1. The angles you collected, each with question, explanation, exercise and check.
2. The sample spread, before and after the peer test.
3. What still needs testing with real beginners.

Keep the individual learning paths in the agreed collection channel, so that minority views and distinctive examples are not lost. Open your pitch by reading your strongest angle aloud or by asking the audience to try the exercise.
