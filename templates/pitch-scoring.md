# Judging criteria

- **Result and demonstration.** Show what your team produced and walk through a real example from your test.
- **Usefulness for LIGHT UP and its readers.** Explain who would use your result and how it improves the comparison.
- **Evidence and reproducibility.** Show your sources, test dates and method. Explain what another team could reproduce and where the evidence is limited.
- **Clarity and timing.** Make the result understandable to a beginner and leave time for questions within your pitch.
