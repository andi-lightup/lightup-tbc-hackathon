# Crypto Exchange Comparison

What must an exchange do for a reader choosing where to buy and withdraw crypto, and how can that reader verify the score?

## Starting point

LIGHT UP compares 59 exchanges across eight categories. Its €1,000 fee test already follows the full route from deposit to withdrawal. Start by finding something a reader still cannot tell from the results. [Published methodology and matrices](https://lightupkryptos.de/so-testen-wir/)

## Inputs to prepare

Bring a dated extract of the matrix, two or three fee schedules and matching observations from the existing fee test if available.

## One possible pilot

For two exchanges, reconstruct what the customer could see before confirming the purchase and what the completed journey actually cost. Show where the spread, trade fee and withdrawal charge enter the calculation. Explain the reference price and timing, and flag any charge you cannot separate. If you only have published prices, label the result as a quote.

**Keeping it current.** Which change in a fee schedule should trigger a new hands-on test? Could a script identify the changed page and send it to an editor for review?

**A question a newsroom could investigate.** Across the exchanges tested, how much of the cost of buying and withdrawing bitcoin could a customer see in advance?

## What to submit

Complete the [comparison submission](../templates/comparison.md). Show one before-and-after matrix entry, results for two or three products, the reader-facing view and the outcome of a peer’s attempt to reproduce one row. Include a maintenance plan and a source a journalist could cite. If you choose a different improvement, use the same deliverables.
