# Rating matrix: Crypto Exchange Comparison

English transcription of the LIGHT UP infosheet (German original: [So testen wir](https://lightupkryptos.de/so-testen-wir/)). The matrix has eight categories: security, costs and transparency, self-custody, usability, savings plan, customer support, offer and staking. An exchange can reach 100 points. Rating = points ÷ 10, so 100 points are a rating of 10.00.

## Security · 27 points

| Criterion | Scoring | Max. |
|---|---|---|
| Registered office | DACH, Luxembourg, Liechtenstein: full points · Europe: 8 · USA: 6 · other reputable jurisdictions (Singapore, Dubai, Israel, Japan): 3 · offshore jurisdictions (Seychelles, Cayman Islands, British Virgin Islands, St Vincent and the Grenadines): 0 | 10 |
| Year founded | Before 2016: full points · after 2024: 0 · 2016 to 2024: pro rata | 6 |
| BaFin or MiCA licence | Yes: full points · no: 0 | 7 |
| Trustpilot | Points based on rating and number of reviews, full weight from 500 reviews | 4 |

## Costs and transparency · 20 points

| Criterion | Scoring | Max. |
|---|---|---|
| Deposit and trading | Cheapest exchange: full points · most expensive: 0 · others: pro rata | 10 |
| Withdrawal | Cheapest exchange: full points · most expensive: 0 · others: pro rata · not possible: 0 | 2 |
| Transparency | Very transparent: full points · transparent: 4 · not transparent: 0 | 8 |

Costs are measured with €1,000 of real money per exchange, from deposit through a bitcoin purchase to withdrawal to a personal wallet.
